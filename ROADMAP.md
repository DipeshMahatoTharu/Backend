## 📝 Whiteboard / Blank-Page Coding Progression
To prepare you for interviews where you must code without autocomplete or starter templates (on paper, whiteboards, or blank editors), we have integrated **Whiteboard/Blank-Page Coding Mode** into your daily files (`whiteboard.py`/`whiteboard.sql`/`whiteboard.md`).

The blank-page difficulty scales up as you progress:
- **Days 20–30**: **20%** of tasks are blank-page coding.
- **Days 31–40**: **30%** of tasks are blank-page coding.
- **Days 41–50**: **40%** of tasks are blank-page coding.
- **Days 51–60**: **50%+** of tasks are blank-page coding.
- **Day 59 (Bootcamp)**: Mock interview simulation (mostly blank-page coding).
- **Day 60 (Capstone)**: 100% blank-page design and implementation (no starter code).
