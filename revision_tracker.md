## ⏱️ The 15-Minute Attempt Rule
1. **Start a 15-minute timer** before looking at any hints or external resources.
2. Read the recall questions and coding challenge in `revision.py`.
3. Try to solve the problem using your own reasoning and verified notes.
4. If you get stuck, use **Hint 1**. Continue reasoning.
5. If still stuck after ~15 minutes, pause and ask your AI Mentor / ChatGPT:
   - *Ask for a conceptual clue or hint first before asking for the complete solution.*
6. After receiving guidance, **re-type and solve the exercise independently**.
7. Record your honest outcome in this tracker:
   - `INDEPENDENT`: Solved without hints or external help.
   - `HINT NEEDED`: Solved after looking at a hint or asking a targeted clarifying question.
   - `REFRESHER NEEDED`: Needed a full conceptual walkthrough before being able to solve it.
   - `NOT ATTEMPTED`: Not yet attempted.
